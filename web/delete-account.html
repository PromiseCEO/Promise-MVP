How to delete your Promise account

In the app

Open Promise → tap Profile → Settings & Account → Delete My Account. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile and Intentional Profile details
  • Your Self Promise
  • Any likes/matches you've sent or received
  • Any couple record you're part of, and the Dating, Engagement, or Marriage Promise documents tied to it
  • Your progress check-ins
  • Your Family Journey data, if applicable

Note: if you're linked to a partner through the Couples or Dating Journey, deleting your account also removes the shared couple record and any promise documents you created together — your partner will need to start fresh if they wish to continue on Promise.

What is not automatically canceled

If you have an active paid subscription, please cancel it separately through the Google Play Store (Play Store app → Menu → Subscriptions) or Apple App Store before or after deleting your account, so you are not charged again. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed through the app stores.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to log in to delete your account through the app, email us at support@thepromiseplatform.com from the email address associated with your account, and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.
